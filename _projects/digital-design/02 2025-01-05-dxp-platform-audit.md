<div class="project-chapter">
    <h3 class="overline case-subsection">[03] Audit</h3>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2001.jpg" alt="Audit overview describing the consolidation of more than 100 corporate websites." loading="lazy">
        <p class="body-regular">
            More than 100 historically grown corporate websites were being brought together as part of an company-wide consolidation. The structures often reflected business logic rather than user needs, while merging sites added further complexity to the user experience.
            <br><br>
            The initial challenge was to understand this landscape, identify problem patterns across sites and user journeys, and turn them into priorities for redesign.
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2002.jpg" alt="Six-category evaluation framework applied across corporate website touchpoints." loading="lazy">
        <p class="body-regular">
            To structure the audit, we started from Nielsen’s established usability heuristics and condensed them into six categories for the project context. This framework provided a consistent basis for reviewing pages, documenting findings and comparing issues across touchpoints.
            <br><br>
            AI-assisted tooling was used to support parts of the analysis and documentation, making the process more structured and efficient.
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2003.jpg" alt="Audit findings grouped into problem clusters and prioritized work packages." loading="lazy">
        <p class="body-regular">
            The evaluation turned 200+ individual findings into a clear, structured view of the platform’s key issues. Recurring patterns were consolidated into 31 defined problem clusters and organized by overarching UX themes.
            <br><br>
            From this, 13 specific work packages were defined and later prioritized for redesign. The framework created a shared foundation for discussing problems and aligning priorities. 
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2004.jpg" alt="Overview of how AI supported audit coverage, pattern finding and synthesis." loading="lazy">
        <p class="body-regular">
            In the workflow, AI tools were purposefully integrated, especially in the audit and synthesis phase. They helped to review more material and structure findings efficiently across a large volume of observations.
            <br><br>
            AI accelerated and expanded the process without replacing critical evaluation. Outputs were reviewed, challenged and refined by the team before informing the analysis.
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2005.jpg" alt="Examples of audit findings covering navigation, search, filters and page hierarchy." loading="lazy">
        <p class="body-regular">
            The audit revealed issues across the overall structure and individual interactions, from complex navigation patterns and inconsistencies to limited user guidance in key processes.
            <br><br>
            These problem patterns helped to build a shared understanding of where the experience needed improvement, as well as aligning stakeholders and the design team on clear priorities.
            <br><br>
            The following examples show how selected findings were translated into concrete design improvements.
        </p>
    </div>
</div>

<div class="project-chapter">
    <h3 class="overline case-subsection">[04] Selected redesign examples</h3>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2006.jpg" alt="Before-and-after comparison showing several navigation systems consolidated into one global architecture." loading="lazy">
        <p class="body-regular">
            The existing platform used four separate navigation systems with overlapping structures and different logic, making orientation increasingly difficult in deeper levels and different site areas. The audit revealed redundant menus, complex nesting, inconsistent hierarchy and unclear navigation patterns. Much of it traced back to internal business structures, surfacing directly in the navigation.
            <br><br>
            The redesign consolidated the navigation into one unified architecture with reduced complexity, clearer grouping and hierarchy, and a consistent global navigation logic. 
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2007.jpg" alt="Responsive product discovery screens combining search and filters across industries, applications and technologies." loading="lazy">
        <p class="body-regular">
            The product portfolio spans hundreds of specialized products across markets, application areas and chemistry categories. Users were confronted with a dense product overview, while a fragmented search and filtering made it difficult to understand available options and narrow them down.
            <br><br>
            The redesign brought search and filtering into a clearer discovery experience, combining cross-area search, visible selection states and multi-dimensional filtering. AND/OR dependencies were translated into a guided flow, helping users narrow down the portfolio step by step.
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2008.jpg" alt="Mobile flow for selecting markets and applications and applying interests to personalized portal content." loading="lazy">
        <p class="body-regular">
            The customer portal provides users with personalized content and information based on their individual interests. The existing process of selecting interests required users to navigate a large set of topics inside of an accordion module, while not giving guidance on how their choices would shape the portal content.
            <br><br>
            The redesign introduced a guided flow to select Markets, review Applications and confirm the selection. User interests became easier to define and manage, and the link between choices and personalized content more transparent. 
        </p>
    </div>
    <div class="project-slide">
        <img src="assets/img/projects/dxp%20platform/DXP%20Slide%2009.jpg" alt="Summary of additional redesigns to the footer, search, filters, contact flow, sharing, language, cards, accessibility and mobile navigation." loading="lazy">
        <div class="body-regular">
            <p>
            Beyond the featured examples, the redesign reached many other parts of the platform, both for desktop and mobile use. The same audit findings applied to smaller flows and interactions, such as a guided contact process. 
            <br><br>
            Additionally, the audit provided the client a structured overview of overall usability issues and recommendations, enabling continuous improvement across the platform.
            </p>
        </div>
    </div>
</div>

<div class="project-chapter">
    <h3 class="overline case-subsection">[05] Outcome & Impact</h3>
    <p class="body-regular">
        What started as a focused audit grew into a broad engagement. The structured findings gave a shared basis for prioritization, and the concepts showed how those findings could become concrete improvements. 
        <br><br>
        The approach and the quality of the work built the client’s confidence and led to follow-up work beyond the original scope.  
        <br><br>
        This project emphasizes the value of grounding redesign decisions in documented problems instead of individual preference. The evidence of the audit process built a shared problem understanding, which made it possible to align on priorities early on.
    </p>
</div>
