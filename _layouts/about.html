I'm a Product Designer based in Hamburg, working at the intersection of user experience, visual design and technology. With a background in Communication Design and several years of agency experience, I combine structured problem-solving and systems thinking with a strong visual foundation.

I take ownership of design challenges from initial problem definition to implementation, connecting user needs, product requirements and technical constraints.
